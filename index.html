About us

Where little minds learn to think fast

Smartkid Abacus Boragaon Centre is a neighbourhood learning centre located at Eurokids, Maligaon Goshala Road, Paschim Boragaon. We help children master numbers using the time-tested abacus method — first with real beads, then with a picture of the abacus in their mind.

Parents tell us what they value most: a nice environment and good teaching staff. That's exactly what we work hard to give every child who walks through our doors.

Why abacus?

More than maths — it's a brain workout

Abacus training engages both hands, eyes and imagination together, strengthening skills children use in every subject.

⚡

Speed & Accuracy

Children learn to add, subtract, multiply and divide quickly — often faster than writing it down.

🎯

Sharper Focus

Bead work and timed practice train attention span and the ability to stay on task.

🧠

Better Memory

Visualising the abacus mentally builds photographic and working memory.

👂

Listening Skills

Listening-based sums teach children to process spoken information carefully.

🌈

Creativity

Mental imagery exercises stretch imagination and creative problem solving.

🏆

Self-Confidence

Every level cleared is a win, helping kids lose the fear of maths for good.

The magic of mental math

From beads in hand to an abacus in the mind

Once children are comfortable with the physical abacus, they begin to "see" it with their eyes closed — moving imaginary beads to solve sums in seconds.
